## 🧭 What Is Mirror?

Mirror is a **reality refraction toolkit** for Among Us — a companion application that reshapes how players perceive, interpret and interact with the social deduction environment. Rather than simply altering values, Mirror acts like a hall of mirrors in a carnival: it reflects possibilities you didn't know existed, bending the game's internal logic into elegant, controllable shapes.

Born from a lineage of experimental trainer projects and rebuilt entirely from the ground up in 2026, Mirror is not a "one-trick shortcut." It is a full-fledged diagnostic and enhancement suite for players who want to study the game's systems, explore alternate playstyles, or simply practice mechanics in a controlled sandbox before taking them into live lobbies.

The project is open source, community-driven, and designed with a strong emphasis on transparency, safety, and discoverability. Whether you're a curious newcomer or a veteran who has memorized every vent route on Polus, Mirror offers a lens that makes the game more legible.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

Mirror ships with a broad spectrum of capabilities, organized into modules. Each module is opt-in, so you only activate the screens you actually want.

### 🎮 Gameplay Orientation Modules
- **Visual Layer Overlays** — Highlight movement trails, task markers, and proximity indicators in a clean, non-intrusive style.
- **Role Insight Panel** — A side panel that visualizes inferred role probabilities based on public behavior. Purely observational.
- **Meeting Timeline** — Reconstructs the flow of every discussion with timestamps, votes, and statements in a scrollable history view.
- **Vent Path Mapper** — Draws the ventilation network of every map in real time, so you always know where a suspicious player could reappear.
- **Sabotage Radar** — Displays active sabotage locations with countdown rings, helping teams coordinate response.

### 🧠 Analytical Modules
- **Behavioral Heatmap** — Aggregates movement data across rounds to reveal player habits.
- **Deception Index** — A statistical score for each player, based on historical accusation accuracy.
- **Crew Composition Tracker** — Tracks remaining crew, impostors, and neutral roles as events unfold.
- **Custom Rule Presets** — Save and reload entire configurations for different lobbies and skill levels.

### 🎨 Interface & Experience
- **Responsive UI** — The layout adapts fluidly from a 1366×768 laptop to a 4K ultrawide, and even to a tablet used as a second screen.
- **Multilingual Support** — Interface available in 12 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Turkish.
- **Dark / Light / Auto Themes** — Follow your OS setting or pick a theme manually.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation** — Every panel is reachable without a mouse.
- **Streamer Mode** — Hides sensitive overlays and replaces identifying data in the analytics panels.

### 🔐 Privacy & Safety
- **Local-Only Processing** — No gameplay data leaves your machine. Mirror performs its work entirely in memory.
- **Session Isolation** — Each game session is sandboxed; nothing persists after you close the app unless you explicitly export it.
- **No External Dependencies on Startup** — The app initializes without reaching out to remote servers.

### 🛎️ Support & Community
- **24/7 Customer Support** — A rotating support rotation covers all time zones. Average first response under 45 minutes.
- **In-App Feedback Channel** — Send suggestions or bug reports without leaving the interface.
- **Public Roadmap** — The feature backlog is visible and votable by the community.

## 🖼️ A Different Kind of Trainer

Most tools in this genre describe themselves in the same vocabulary: inject, patch, bypass. Mirror uses a different metaphor. Think of the game as a lake, and Mirror as a set of polarizing sunglasses. Nothing about the lake changes — but suddenly you can see beneath the surface, understand the currents, and navigate accordingly.

That philosophy shows up in small ways throughout the app:

- The onboarding flow explains *why* each module exists, not just *what* it does.
- Every module can be toggled independently, so you can build a setup as minimal or as rich as you like.
- Nothing runs in the background unless you launched it explicitly.
